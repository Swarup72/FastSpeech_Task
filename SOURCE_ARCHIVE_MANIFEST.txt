6. FILES NECESSARY FOR DEBUGGING BUT EXCLUDED (AND EXACT REFERENCE DETAILS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following dataset, checkpoint, and normalization assets were excluded to keep the
source-code archive lightweight and portable, but are critical for acoustic debugging:

A. Feature Normalization Statistics (preprocessed_data/Svarah/stats.json):
   Pitch and energy normalization statistics computed strictly on the training partition.
   Inference relies on these statistics to denormalize pitch and energy predictions.
   Verbatim content of stats.json:
   {
     "pitch": [-2.9928221702575684, 10.125201225280762, 177.93157413802922, 59.4527742349165],
     "energy": [-0.8997657299041748, 7.464303493499756, 27.314464756296125, 30.357307854024445],
     "phoneme_level": {
       "pitch": [179.8254725405027, 58.399375282872754],
       "energy": [30.58348727790848, 28.531268854647962]
     }
   }
   [Note: format is [min, max, mean, std] for frame-level, and [mean, std] for phoneme-level].

B. Speaker Demographic ID Mapping (preprocessed_data/Svarah/speakers.json):
   Maps demographic profile IDs (spk_000 to spk_114) to integer IDs (0 to 114).
   The full demographic metadata table (gender, age group, native language) is
   fully included in the archive at English/metadata/speaker_profiles.json.

C. Trained Model Checkpoint:
   * Path: /content/drive/MyDrive/FastSpeech2_Training/checkpoints/Svarah_profile_stratified_v1/checkpoint_best.pth.tar
   * Size: ~399.08 MB (step=19000, epoch=100, val_loss=2.3489003153).
   * Local diagnostic mock: checkpoints/Svarah_profile_stratified_v1/checkpoint_best_diagnostic_mock.pth.tar.bak

D. HiFi-GAN Vocoder Pretrained Weights:
   * Path: original_fastspeech2/hifigan/generator_universal.pth.tar (55.8 MB) / generator_universal.pth.tar.zip (51.9 MB)
   * Vocoder model architecture (original_fastspeech2/hifigan/models.py) and config (config.json) are included.
